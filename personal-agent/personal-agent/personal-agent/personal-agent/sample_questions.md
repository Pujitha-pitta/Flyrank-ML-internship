# Sample Questions

These are example questions that the AI Portfolio Assistant should be able to answer.

## About Me
- Who is Naga Pujitha?
- Tell me about Naga Pujitha.
- Give me a summary of Pujitha's background.

## Education
- Where did Pujitha study?
- What degree does Pujitha have?
- Tell me about Pujitha's education.

## Technical Skills
- What programming languages does Pujitha know?
- What machine learning frameworks has Pujitha used?
- What tools is Pujitha familiar with?

## Projects
- What projects has Pujitha completed?
- Explain the FlyRank ML Internship project.
- Tell me about the Diabetes Prediction project.
- What was the Bitcoin Price Prediction project about?
- Describe Pujitha's portfolio website.

## Research
- What is Pujitha's master's thesis about?
- What research areas has Pujitha worked on?

## Internship
- What did Pujitha do during the FlyRank internship?
- What machine learning experience does Pujitha have?

## Career
- What roles is Pujitha interested in?
- Why should I hire Pujitha?
- What are Pujitha's career interests?

## Contact
- Where can I find Pujitha's GitHub?
- Where can I view Pujitha's portfolio?
- How can I learn more about Pujitha's work?
